Privacy Policy — Baccarat APT v6

Last updated: July 2026

1. Overview

Baccarat APT v6 ("the App") is a statistical analysis tool for the card game Baccarat. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

2. Data We Collect

The App collects no personal data. All data is stored locally on your device:

  • Game history (P/B/T results) — stored in browser localStorage
  • Session statistics — stored in browser localStorage
  • Shoe comparison database — stored in browser localStorage
  • Bankroll settings — stored in browser localStorage

None of this data is transmitted to any server.

3. AI Assistant

When you use the AI Assistant feature, your shoe statistics (P/B/T ratios, counts, patterns) are sent to Anthropic's API for analysis. No personal information is included in these requests. Please review Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

4. Microphone Access

The voice input feature requires microphone access. Audio is processed locally by your device's speech recognition system. We do not record, store, or transmit audio data.

5. No Gambling

Baccarat APT v6 is a statistical analysis and educational tool. It does not facilitate real-money gambling, does not process payments, and does not connect to any casino or gambling platform.

6. Children

This app is not intended for users under 18 years of age.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of changes.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the developer via Google Play Store.
